## Authority and use boundary

The courseware paraphrases ISO 14001 concepts for learning. It does not reproduce the copyrighted standard, provide legal advice, or replace a licensed ISO 14001:2026 copy. Official ISO and Singapore authority sources take precedence for current status and obligations; implementation guides are explanatory secondary sources.
